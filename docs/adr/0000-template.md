# ADR template

Copy to `NNNN-kebab-title.md`, number sequentially, status one of
`proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | rejected`.

```md
# NNNN — Title in the imperative

Status: accepted (YYYY-MM-DD)

## Context

What problem or tension forced a decision. Concrete example or numbers if they exist.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two sentences, then the specifics.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative with the reason it lost.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must be revisited and when.
```
